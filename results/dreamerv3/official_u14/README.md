**ataripy seed 7**: a single 27,001-agent-step episode (the 108k-frame time limit; the agent stalled at

What it says:
- No factor varied here detectably moves the official code: fp32 against bf16 on H100 gives Welch t = 0.05;
  atari-py t = -0.52; A100 1/5 is too few seeds to call (p = 0.10 against the H100 batch).
- The published 4/5 is consistent with the official code's rate here (Fisher p = 0.15). The original run's 0/5
  was the unlucky tail: against the next 15 H100 seeds, p = 0.016.
- Our port against all official: breakouts p = 0.33, mean Welch t = 1.80. Lower point estimates, not significant.
